# Proof of Life

**Category:** Forensics
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Author:** Andy Roid

## Description

The relay ICARUS's navigator phones home to is still answering. It won't talk
to anyone who can't prove they're its implant — but you pulled the implant's
key material out of the last session. Become the implant: complete its
check-in, satisfy whatever the handler asks, and take down the tasking it was
waiting for. The flag is in that tasking — and so is where it's been hiding on
the Keeper of Tomorrow's reactor controller.

## Connection

Relay: `<EVENT_HOST:PORT>` (HTTPS, JSON, `POST /relay/checkin`)

No files. You'll need what you recovered in Prompt Autopsy.
