# Cafeteria Menu

Welcome to **The Mess Hall**, RASA's premier (and only) dining facility on this station. All meals are prepared fresh*, from ingredients grown in our own hydroponics bay or shipped in from Earth at great expense.

<small>*"Fresh" is defined as "rehydrated within the last 24 hours."</small>

## Cafeteria Rules

1. **Label your food in the shared fridge.** Unlabeled items are considered communal after 48 hours.
2. **No crumbs near the air vents.** Crumbs in low gravity go *everywhere*.
3. **Clean up after yourself.** Your mother does not work here.
4. **Do not microwave fish.** This is a closed air system. Your crewmates will find you.
5. **Badges stay out of sight.** Even at lunch. See **Workplace Safety**.
