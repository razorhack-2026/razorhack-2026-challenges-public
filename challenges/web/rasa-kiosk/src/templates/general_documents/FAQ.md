# Frequently Asked Questions

New to RASA? You're probably wondering some of these.

## General

**What does RASA actually do?**
We mine asteroids, moons, and the occasional comet for metals, water ice, and other raw materials, then ship them where they're needed. See **Welcome to RASA** for the full overview.

**What do the badge colors mean?**

| Stripe | Role |
|---|---|
| Red | Flight Crew |
| Green | Sponsor |
| Purple | Gamemaster |

**I'm new. Am I expected to know how to do everything?**
Absolutely not. You're here to learn. Ask questions early and often.

**Who should I go to when I'm stuck?**
Start with your crew. If you're still stuck, find a Gamemaster. They won't hand you the answer, but they'll point you in the right direction.

## Badges & Access

**Why can't I open some documents?**
Different badges have different access levels. Flight Crew can see the General library. Some documents are restricted to Gamemasters and Sponsors.

**My badge isn't scanning. What do I do?**
Make sure it's held flat, well lit, and fully in view of the scanner. Remove anything covering it, like a sticker or a thumb. If it still won't scan, contact the Badge Office.

**I lost my badge. Is that a big deal?**
Yes. Report it right away so it can be deactivated. See **Workplace Safety** for why.

**Can I post a picture of my badge? It looks really cool.**
No. We know it looks cool. Still no. See **Workplace Safety**.

## Working Here

**How long are shifts?**
Long enough that you should take breaks. Seriously, take breaks.

**Can I use AI tools to help me?**
You can use whatever helps you *learn*. Using a tool to explain a concept is great. Using it to skip the thinking entirely means you're missing out on the whole point of being here.

**How do I connect to the station's machines?**
Over SSH. The **Connection** guide walks you through it on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

**I found some weird text that looks like gibberish. What is it?**
Possibly nothing, possibly something encoded. The **How To Read** guide covers the common formats and how to decode them.

## Station Life

**Is the cafeteria food any good?**
Read the **Cafeteria Menu** and decide for yourself.

**What's that humming noise?**
Probably life support. If it stops, that's when you should worry.

**Is there Wi-Fi?**
Yes, but it's routed through a relay satellite, so please don't stream video during ore transfers.

**What happens if I see something move outside the window?**
There's a hotline for that. Check **Workplace Safety**.
