# Welcome to Administration

Congratulations on your promotion! RASA is so proud to see you take initiative and move up in your role on the team! Before you get too far ahead of yourself, here are some pointers to get you comfortable with your new responsibilities.

## What Changes Now

As an administrator, you can now see things most of the crew can't. With that comes a few new expectations:

- **You have access to restricted documents.** Everything in this library is for Gamemasters and Sponsors only. Don't share it with Flight Crew, no matter how nicely they ask.
- **You manage badges.** Issuing, updating, and revoking crew badges is now part of your job. Read **Badge Administration Procedures** before you touch the kiosk.
- **You log incidents.** If something goes wrong on your watch, it goes in the **Incident Log**. Big or small.
- **You're a role model.** New cadets will watch how you work. If you leave your badge out at lunch, so will they.

## Your Badge Is Now a Bigger Target

Your old Flight Crew badge opened a few doors. Your new one opens *a lot* more. That makes it far more valuable to anyone who wants access they shouldn't have.

Everything in **Workplace Safety** still applies, only more so:

1. Never photograph your badge or let outsiders get a good look at it.
2. Keep it out of sight when you leave work areas.
3. Never lend it out, even to another administrator.
4. Report it immediately if it goes missing.

## Helping the Crew

A big part of being a Gamemaster or Sponsor is helping cadets learn. A few tips:

- **Nudge, don't solve.** When a cadet is stuck, ask them questions that point them in the right direction instead of giving them the answer.
- **Encourage the struggle.** Being stuck for a while is how people learn. Let them work through it.
- **Be approachable.** A cadet who's afraid to ask for help will usually just give up.
- **Remind them to take breaks.** Tired cadets make mistakes, and so do tired administrators.

## Your First Week in Administration

1. Read every document in this library, all the way to the end.
2. Shadow an experienced administrator during at least one badge issuance.
3. Review the latest **Quarterly Operations Report** so you know where the company stands.
4. Introduce yourself to the other Gamemasters and Sponsors.
5. Find out who keeps eating the pudding. (Optional, but highly encouraged.)

## A Final Word

You got here by being curious, paying attention, and not giving up. Keep doing that. Administration isn't the end of the learning, just the next level of it.

Welcome to the big leagues.
