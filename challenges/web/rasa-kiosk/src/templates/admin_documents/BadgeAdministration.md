# Badge Administration Procedures

**Restricted: Gamemaster and Sponsor access only.**

As an administrator, you're responsible for issuing, updating, and revoking crew badges. This document outlines standard procedure.

## Badge Types

| Role | Stripe Color | Access Level |
|---|---|---|
| Flight Crew | Red | General |
| Sponsor | Green | Admin |
| Gamemaster | Purple | Admin |

Every badge has three things the kiosk checks:

1. The **stripe color** matches the **printed role label**.
2. The **access level** matches what the holder is registered at.
3. The **printed name** matches the name on the holder's registered badge.

All three must agree. If any one of them is wrong, the badge is rejected.

## Issuing a New Badge

1. Confirm the new crew member's identity in person.
2. Register them at the kiosk using **Add New User**. This records their face and badge together.
3. Double-check the name is spelled exactly as printed on the card.
4. Hand over the badge and remind them about the badge rules in **Workplace Safety**. Yes, even the photo one. *Especially* the photo one.

## Updating a Badge

Use **Update Info** when a crew member is promoted, changes roles, or has a damaged card replaced. The kiosk will ask for:

1. Their face
2. Their current badge
3. Their new badge

The name on the new badge must match the old one.

## Lost or Stolen Badges

- Treat every lost badge as compromised until proven otherwise.
- Deactivate it immediately and issue a replacement.
- Log the incident in the **Incident Log**.
- If the badge was photographed or seen by outsiders before it went missing, flag it for Security review.

## Things to Watch For

- Badges where the stripe color is *almost* right but not quite
- Names printed in a slightly different font than the standard card
- Crew members who are oddly protective about letting you see their badge up close
- Crew members who are oddly *eager* to show you a badge you've never seen before

When in doubt, ask for their face scan. Faces are much harder to print.
