# Zero-G Salvage

**Category:** Web
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Author:** Lt. Rico Kopernicus

## Description

The Icarus just put a shot straight through our displacement drive!
Without it, there's no way we're jumping out of this solar system any
time soon. Drifting on what's left of our reserves, we've limped into
range of an independent salvage exchange that runs out here:
Zero-G Salvage — commodities, salvaged parts, the works.

We caught a break: the only working replacement part in the sector just
surfaced on the exchange's listings — a sealed "temporal anomaly"
artifact that reads, unmistakably, as a displacement-drive housing.
We have a little bit of steel and iron stores in our hold, and Zero-G
gives all new accounts a starting bonus of 5,000 credits. That alone
won't cover the cost of buying that part though.

Find a way to earn enough credits to buy it and repair our ship!
