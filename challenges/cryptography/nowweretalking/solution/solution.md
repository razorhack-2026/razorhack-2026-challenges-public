This challenge provides participants two anomaly reports based on CPU relinquishment from the SAS (security Access System). The two anomaly reports provided show two important pieces of information: A synopsis of the data at the top of the page, and the actual data recorded concerning the cores of the CPU. These two pieces of information are important because the synopsis mentions which core was anomalous (so participants can confirm which core they should investigate) and the data is key in decoding the necessary message. 
In order to decode the message from each report, the first thing participants should look at is the processes associated with each anomaly detected. For example, the first process of the two chronologically is process 4417, so it would be wise for participants to work on this report first. Then they can move on to the second report (process 5289). 
When the participants work on the first report, they will notice a specific pattern in the second core. Each sample per 100 ms is either incredibly high or incredibly low. Typically, this would indicate a sort of binary system, where the high samples can be interpreted as 1s and the low samples as 0s. Participants may first try this, but they will find that it does not produce any readable message. They may be stumped! Instead, they should interpret the message counterintuitively: the high samples are 0s, and the low samples are 1s. If participants are still stuck on this, they can unlock the hint on CTFd about Vic and her black mold problem!
Participants should group the bits into groups of 8 and translate each 8 bits to ASCII. For example, on the first flagged process, core 2 starts with “98, 7, 95, 12, 92, 7, 99, 87”. That translates to 01010100 (with the 0s and the 1s reversed as previously explained). Participants can look up an ASCII table to see that 01010100 translates to “T”, which is the first letter of the message. They should do this for each sample of the process to get the first half of the message. Then, they will go to the second report and do the same thing for the first core. 
When participants end with the message “THE TEMP IN DAVIES JOURNAL”, they should confront Davie Bowman about this and ask her about the temp mentioned in the mysterious, covert conversation. Davie will give them the flag, which they will input to unlock a series of other challenges related to sensitive information and blackmailing.
