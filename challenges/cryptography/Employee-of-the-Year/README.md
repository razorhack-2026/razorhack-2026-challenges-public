# Employee of the Year - Kalium Collector

**Category:** Cryptography
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Author:** WALL-D
**Series:** KALIUM mining, part 3 of 3 (DCU Mole-03)

## Description

    Hey!

    Thank you again for helping extract those KALIUM deposits. It's nice to see our company values so thoughtfully reflected in an employee! I've been getting a ton of messages from the Extraction Division as well highlighting your efforts, and I will be sure to have this noted in your performance review at the end of the year. Now that you have a bit of experience, I'd like to ask you to help out one more time. On NOVARE, we not only found KALIUM, we found a mother-load deposit. The more we mine, the larger the vein seems to grow. Typically, we would have multiple units working on this deposit at once, but, given the circumstance, we're moving everyone who has manually mined a deposit onto this one to not maximize productivity and shareholder investments. This deposit is a bit harder than the last, and we're only mining one section at a time, so once a team has input the correct DIS, the drill will lock out until we reach the next section. I hope to see good work from you again, and thank you for all you've done!

    Your humble HR representative,
    WALL-D

    [A new block to mine will appear every 30 minutes. Once a team mines the block, it is locked out for everybody until the next block appears. Scoring: Team's Score * 100 (if you solve 5 blocks, you get 500 points)]

## Files Provided

None. This challenge is hosted on one THE-DRILL shared by every team, so all teams race for
the same block: SSH in as `TEAM#XX` (your team number) at the address in the challenge's
connection info, with the password the challenge shows your team, and select **DRILL CONTAINMENT
UNIT Mole-03** (unit 3) from the Access Console. The challenge opens on the scoreboard once your
team has mined Dog-02 (High Value Deposit).
