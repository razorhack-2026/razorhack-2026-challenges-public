# Remote Mining

**Category:** Cryptography
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Author:** WALL-D
**Series:** KALIUM mining, part 1 of 3 (DCU Termite-01)

## Description

     KALIUM is a very particular resource. It requires careful attention when being harvested: the drills must maneuver around the intricacies of its surface in order to safely extract the resource without breaking it apart. In the chaos of this. . . _incident_, it seems like the systems we had at RazorPower Co to automate its harvest have fallen short. With that said, we have decided to bring back the classic method from when we first discovered the power source: manual mining! That's right, right now, YOU can connect to THE-DRILL (hosting our state of the art mining machines) and help us continue to extract this valuable resource! Remember, not only is the future of humanity in your hands, but any additional ore mined might factor into your performance bonus at the end of the year!

     Thank you for your help and happy mining,
     RazorPower Co - Extraction Division

## Files Provided

None. This challenge is hosted on THE-DRILL, your team's own copy: SSH in as `TEAM#XX` (your team
number) with the address, port and password in the challenge's connection info, and select **DRILL CONTAINMENT UNIT Termite-01** (unit 1) from the Access Console.
