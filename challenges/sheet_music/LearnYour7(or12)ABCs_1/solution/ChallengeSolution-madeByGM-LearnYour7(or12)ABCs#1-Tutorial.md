## ChallengeName  
Learn Your 7 (or 12) ABCs #1 - Tutorial  

**Category:**    Steganography / Sheet Music  
**Difficulty:**  training  
**Author:**      "Mort"  

### ChallengeDescription_Themed_ViewableByParticipant  
Heard by ear and transcribed to sheet music, a security officer had a hunch that note names present in the sheet music covertly sound the song of the crew's flag. Can you verify their hunch?  

### FilesProvidedToParticipant

* LearnYour7(or12)ABCs_1-Tutorial.pdf  

### Flags that participant can submit

## Solution
1) Noticeable quirk about the data: Listening to the recording makes it seem like each measure is just a variation of the same overall theme.  
2) Technique: Get name of each note within the first measure. Requires participant being aware that typical note names are A,B,C,D,E,F,G, and where (vertically) those note names exist on the Treble clef staff.  
3) Flag Recovery: The first 7 note names as a string is the flag.  

### Known Issues / Notes  
1) The 1st and 2nd measures provide the flag directly and easily, but the following measures can also give you the flag if you're an expert in interpreting chords (I am not, but Lilypond software allows easy transposition).  
2) There are very few complexities here, with no chords/voices, no changing tempos, equivalent durations (quarter) for all notes, but there is use of a fermata, vertical repeat bars starting and ending in the 2nd measure, and a single sharp in the transposed measure.
