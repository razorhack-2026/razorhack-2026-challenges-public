# Lateral Drift

**Category:** ICS
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Author:** Voyager 1

*(2/3 of the "Lateral Drift" sequence - requires the credential recovered in **Know Your
Mark**.)*

## Description

The VPN credential you talked out of Rootkit only gets you onto RazorPower Co.'s corporate
segment, nothing further. Somewhere on that box is what you need to reach the engineering
network, and somewhere past that, deep in the KEEPER OF TOMORROW's engineering network, is a
panel nobody locked down properly.

## Files Provided

None - this is a live-service challenge. The host and port are shown on the challenge page;
use the credential from Know Your Mark.

## Connection

SSH to the `vpn-gateway` host with the credential recovered in Know Your Mark.
