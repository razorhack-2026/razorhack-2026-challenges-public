3. Maintenance lock

The controller ships locked. Until the maintenance unlock code is written to 40004, setpoint writes are ignored and the value reads back unchanged. Factory default unlock code: {{ unlock_code }}. Five wrong codes in a row lock register 40004 for 60 seconds.

4. Rotor protection

The firmware limits the setpoint to safe_limit (40002). Bearing wear (40008) builds up while vibration (40006) is high and recovers when it is low. If the bearing wears out (100 %), the rotor seizes, the cascade scram relay latches, and the draw-off from the feed vessel stops. The relay stays latched until the controller is restarted. Without the draw-off the feed vessel temperature (40009) rises.

Operating note: vibration is not constant across the speed range. Like any rotor, the RC-200 has a critical speed somewhere in its working range, and the vendor advises keeping it away from that speed. The critical speed moves as the bearing heats up.
